SOURCE: KOHLER official support pages

For product support:
- Locate the product model number before requesting support when possible.
- KOHLER provides product support, warranty information, installation guidance and troubleshooting resources.
- KOHLER Assist provides tools for finding products/parts, model numbers and installation guides.
- KOHLER's support contact form can accept photos/documents and asks for product type and model number.

Official support:
https://assist.kohler.com/en
https://www.kohler.com/en/support/contact

Product identification:
KOHLER says its Scout app can help identify a product model number from an uploaded photo. This prototype does not claim to reproduce Scout's identification accuracy.
