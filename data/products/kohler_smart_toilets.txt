KOHLER Smart Toilets
KOHLER's smart toilet range includes models such as Veil, Leap, Innate, Eir, Numi 2.0 and others. Features vary by model.

General smart-toilet guidance:
- Smart toilets may provide automatic flushing, heated seats, bidet cleansing, lighting, deodorizing and other features depending on model.
- KOHLER's current smart toilet guidance says installation requires water supply and a nearby grounded electrical outlet for powered features.
- Many floor-mount smart toilets are designed around a 12-inch rough-in, but the exact model must be checked before installation.
- Before purchase or installation, verify rough-in measurement, outlet location, available floor space and lid clearance.
- Use the exact model's installation guide for model-specific requirements.
