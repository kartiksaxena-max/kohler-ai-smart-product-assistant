PURPOSE: Prototype knowledge base. Exact operation varies by model; verify against the model manual.

Smart toilet operation:
- Some KOHLER smart toilets can be operated with a paired remote control and/or a control panel.
- Some models support automatic flushing when the feature is enabled.
- Some models provide a manual flush button.
- One KOHLER technical guide describes eco and full flush options, but the exact water volume differs by model.
- Do not assume a remote-control button layout or water volume applies to every KOHLER smart toilet.

Example from KOHLER technical documentation:
- A manual flush button can provide flushing without the remote/control panel on supported models.
- Some models use automatic flush after the user leaves or the cover is lowered when automatic flush is enabled.
